A unique piece of geology, this is a large cabinet sized piece of basalt with inclusions of peridotite, which are ripped from the Earth's mantle during energetic volcanic events. Peridotite is made of olivine, pyroxenes and sometimes garnets and are the large green blocks suspended in the black basalt. This piece is 20 x 15 x 14 cm, weighs 3615 grams and was collected in San Carlos Volcanic Field, Arizona, USA. It is an incredible piece of geology and an excellent conversation starter if used as home decor!
